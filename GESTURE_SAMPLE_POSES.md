# Gesture recognition reference

MakaLearn uses MediaPipe and the trained seven-label recognizer, not the earlier rule-based pose demo. Supported signs are I want to go to toilet, I want to eat, I want to drink, Help, Yes, No, and Sit down.

Use the reference materials in Gesture practice. Uploading another gesture material does not train or extend recognition. Camera frames remain on the device. Optional AI feedback receives structured recognition results only. Gesture attempts are never saved.

See [the current system reference](docs/current-system.md) for current behavior. The original temporary pose mappings are retained only in [the historical demo note](docs/archive/gesture-sample-poses-history.md).

Learning references are placeholders and are not official Makaton teaching or assessment material.
